=== POST 003 ===
The trading debate that never dies: tight stop-losses vs. riding out volatility.

Run tight -1.5% stops on TSLA or PLTR, and intraday liquidity sweeps clip your stop right before the bounce.

Drop stops completely, and one regime shift erases six months of gains.

In quant portfolios, the fix usually isn't tighter price stops. It's sizing positions inversely to 20-day volatility (1/sigma) before entry. Which side are you on?
